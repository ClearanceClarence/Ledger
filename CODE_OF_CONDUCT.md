# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

I want Ledger to be a project people can contribute to and use without dealing with hostility. That means treating each other with respect, regardless of background, experience level, identity, or any of the usual axes people get treated badly along.

## Standards

Things that help:

- Being patient with newcomers and people learning
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback without making it personal
- Owning mistakes, apologizing, and moving on
- Focusing on what's best for the project and the people using it
- Disagreeing about technical decisions without being a jerk about it

Things that don't help:

- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Insults, personal attacks, or derogatory comments
- Trolling, baiting, or deliberately inflammatory behavior
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Anything else that a reasonable person would consider unprofessional

## Scope

This applies in any project-related space — issues, pull requests, discussions, the codebase itself, and any direct communication identifiable as part of the project. It also applies when someone is officially representing Ledger publicly (in talks, posts, etc.).

## Enforcement

If someone violates the code of conduct, report it to the maintainer privately through one of:

- A direct message to the maintainer through their GitHub profile
- Email (address on the maintainer's GitHub profile)

Reports will be taken seriously and handled confidentially. I'll respond within a few days. Depending on the severity, possible outcomes include:

1. **A private conversation** explaining what was wrong and asking for an apology or change in behavior
2. **A public correction** if the behavior was public
3. **A temporary ban** from project spaces
4. **A permanent ban** for severe or repeated violations

I'll use my judgment. If you think a decision was wrong, you can appeal by replying to the original message — I'll think it over and may change my mind.

## Maintainer accountability

I'm not exempt from this. If I'm being a jerk, call me out. If it's serious enough that you don't feel comfortable doing that directly, reach out through any channel you trust and I'll take it seriously.
